# Docs

The docs are in the order you'll need them: getting devvm running, using it day to day, staying safe and choosing a backend, then every command, how devvm works and how to change it.

- [Getting started](/docs/getting-started/index.md): Installing devvm, making your first VM, and the commands you use most.
- [Using devvm](/docs/using/index.md): Images and toolchains, ports and size, coding agents, editors, settings, and what to do when something fails.
- [Staying safe](/docs/safety/index.md): The habits that keep you safe, network tiers, `.git` protection, and what devvm does about each attack.
- [Backends](/docs/backends/index.md): What runs the VMs: Lima.
- [CLI reference](/docs/cli/index.md): Every command, with its arguments and flags.
- [How it works](/docs/internals/index.md): How devvm builds images, forwards ports, confines VMs to their proxies and protects `.git`.
- [Contributing](/docs/contributing/index.md): Building, testing and writing docs, other hosts, and releases.
